# Ideas

The level pages show **how** each sample works. These essays are about **why** it matters: what it
means to put a thought into a machine, what goes wrong when a system speaks without knowing what its
words commit it to, and why the most impressive reasoning systems of recent years pair a neural
network with a logic that checks it.

They assume no Hilbert, and little logic. Read them before the samples, or after, or instead.

| Essay | The question it answers |
|---|---|
| [Putting a thought into a machine](thinking-machines.md) | Why is simulating belief, knowledge and intention hard, and how do possible worlds solve it? |
| [The modal systems, explained](modal-systems.md) | What are K, T, D, S4, S5 and KD45, and how do you choose one for a real problem? |
| [When a chatbot makes a promise](semantics-and-accountability.md) | Why did an airline pay for its chatbot's answer, and what kinds of problem does semantics solve? |
| [Neuro-symbolic AI](neuro-symbolic.md) | Why does a network that proposes plus a logic that checks beat chain-of-thought on its own? |
